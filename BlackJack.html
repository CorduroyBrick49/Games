House rules

Table 7 deals from a six-deck shoe under these rules.

Shoe
Six 52-card decks. A cut card sits about 75% of the way in; the shoe is reshuffled once it comes out.
Payouts
Blackjack pays 3 to 2. Other wins pay 1 to 1. Insurance pays 2 to 1.
Dealer
Draws to 16 and stands on all 17s, including soft 17. Checks for blackjack when showing an ace or a ten-value card.
Doubling
Allowed on any first two cards, including after a split. A doubled hand receives exactly one card.
Splitting
Any two cards of equal value, up to four hands. Split aces receive one card each. A 21 after a split pays 1 to 1.
Surrender
Not offered.
Limits
$5 minimum and $500 maximum per hand.
